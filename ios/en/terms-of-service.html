6-1. Stories saved on the device

In the iOS app you can save free stories and stories you own through credit-purchased access rights to the device and read them without an internet connection. Owned stories can be saved only while signed in to a parent account; in account-free story mode you can save and remove free stories only. Paid stories without an access right cannot be saved.

Saved copies are for private viewing inside the Ddukddak Tales app only. Do not copy or extract their files out of the app or send, distribute or share them; section 6 also applies. Saving does not give ownership of story files.

Saved free stories open in parent mode and story mode on that device. Saved owned stories open only while the account that saved them is signed in on that device (including when the sign-in cannot be checked because there is no connection); after sign-out or switching to story mode they stay hidden until that account signs in again. When another account signs in, the previous account’s owned copies are deleted from that device.

While online, the app checks that each copy can still be opened. If 30 days pass since the last check, the copy cannot be opened until you connect and it is checked again. An owned copy stops opening once that account can no longer view the story online (for example, access revoked after an Apple refund, or the story is withdrawn); a free copy stops opening once the story is no longer designated free or is withdrawn. If a later check confirms this, the copy is deleted from the device.

Saved copies are not updated automatically; when the app shows that a story has new content, you can download it again as described above.

Copies are kept only in the device’s cache area and are not included in iCloud or device backups. If storage runs low, iOS may delete them; such copies leave the list and can be downloaded again. Copies use device storage, and downloading may incur data charges. Remove copies on the Saved on this device screen (story mode removes free copies only); deleting the app also removes them, and deleting your account in the app removes all copies on that device.

The first subscription period is one month from the first payment. Until you cancel, the same price is charged to your Apple account at the end of each period and a new period starts. There is no introductory price or free trial, and Family Sharing does not apply.
  2. The subscription starts after the store confirms payment. Pending, cancelled or failed payments do not give access.
  3. During a subscription period you can use every paid story the service provides, including stories released during that period, with all of their prepared elements. Features that cost money each time they are used may have separate limits, as in section 4. Stories opened only through the subscription cannot be saved to the device (section 6-1).
  4. The subscription does not give story access rights. When it ends, paid stories without an access right lock again; reading progress, credit balances and access rights you bought stay. You can buy credits and unlock (own) stories with credits while subscribed, and your credit balance stays.
  5. You can cancel at any time in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions on your device or with Manage subscription in the app. Cancelling stops the next charge, and you can keep using the subscription until the end of the period you paid for. Change your payment method in your Apple account.

To raise the price for current subscribers we give notice under applicable law and Apple’s procedure and, where consent is required, obtain it through Apple’s procedure. If you do not consent, you are not charged the higher price and the subscription ends at the end of the current period. A lower price applies from the next renewal.

  9. While an account is subscribed through one store (Google Play or the App Store), it cannot start a new subscription through the other. If you end up subscribed through both, subscription periods are not combined or extended; cancel the one you do not want in the store you used. Refunds for the duplicate payment can be requested under that store’s subscription refund rules (Google Play: article 6-7 of the Android/web terms; App Store: Apple’s process in section 6-3), and support explains how to request them.

We stop selling the subscription so that it does not renew after the end date, explain how to request a refund through Apple’s refund process for any paid subscription period that can no longer be used, and help with the confirmation needed.
