6-1. Stories saved on the device

From app version 1.0.1, you can save free stories and stories you own through credit-purchased access rights to the device and read them without an internet connection. Owned stories can be saved only while signed in to a parent account; in account-free story mode you can save and remove free stories only. Paid stories without an access right cannot be saved.

Saved copies are for private viewing inside the Ddukddak Tales app only. Do not copy or extract their files out of the app or send, distribute or share them; section 6 also applies. Saving does not give ownership of story files.

Saved free stories open in parent mode and story mode on that device. Saved owned stories open only while the account that saved them is signed in on that device (including when the sign-in cannot be checked because there is no connection); after sign-out or switching to story mode they stay hidden until that account signs in again. When another account signs in, the previous account’s owned copies are deleted from that device.

While online, the app checks that each copy can still be opened. If 30 days pass since the last check, the copy cannot be opened until you connect and it is checked again. An owned copy stops opening once that account can no longer view the story online (for example, access revoked after an Apple refund, or the story is withdrawn); a free copy stops opening once the story is no longer designated free or is withdrawn. If a later check confirms this, the copy is deleted from the device.

Saved copies are not updated automatically; when the app shows that a story has new content, you can download it again.

Copies are kept only in the device’s cache area and are not included in iCloud or device backups. If storage runs low, iOS may delete them; such copies leave the list and can be downloaded again. Copies use device storage, and downloading may incur data charges. Remove copies on the Saved stories screen (story mode removes free copies only); deleting the app also removes them, and deleting your account in the app removes all copies on that device.

This section takes effect on November 24, 2026 (announced November 17, 2026) and applies from when the iOS app (1.0.1 or later) starts offering device saving on or after that date.
