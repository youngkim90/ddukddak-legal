Ddukddak Studio processes personal information for the Ddukddak Tales iOS app as described here. This page does not amend the existing Android/web policy or its notice schedule. A parent account can share balances, story access and usage records across platforms.

1. Data and purposes

  • Accounts: email, a securely managed password, nickname and internal account ID. Email or Google, Kakao and Apple account identifiers and supplied profile data support signup, sign-in and deletion.
  • Apple sign-in: Apple identifier, shared/relay email, first supplied name when available and authentication results. A token issued for revoking Apple authorization may be stored encrypted on our server. We attempt revocation when the account is deleted. Support does not ask for your Apple password or verification code.
  • Reading and usage: story/page progress, recent stories, login times/dates, language and attendance dates support reading continuation, notification language and attendance rewards.
  • Apple credit purchases: app-account token, product/transaction IDs, price/currency, purchase/grant/finish/refund states and times and signed purchase-verification data support verification, exactly-once credit grants, refunds, fraud prevention and disputes. Apple processes payment-method details; we do not collect card numbers.
  • Credits and story access: grants, spending, expiration, refund adjustments, opened stories and revoked access. Existing purchases on another platform may be processed when their balance or story access is used with the same account.
  • Push: if authorized and used, Expo push token, iOS/APNs delivery data, app/OS, language and consent state support service notifications. Disable notifications in app settings.
  • Support and feedback: message/attachments, reply email, selected category/rating, related story/page/language, app version, OS/device and transaction identifiers supplied for purchase support.
  • Diagnostics and updates: parent-mode session/error/crash data, app/device/OS, surrounding screen/action context, a random installation identifier, access information and update/runtime identifiers support reliability and update delivery. We do not use them for advertising tracking.

2. Story mode and local storage

Children under 14 do not create accounts directly. Account-free story mode offers designated stories; account, purchase and support features are in parent mode. A birth date is used to determine an age band and is not stored. Age band, language and preferences are stored locally; sign-in sessions use the operating system’s secure storage. Account functions and diagnostic transmission are restricted in child/unknown-age mode. Content delivery can still expose IP/request information to network providers; we do not promise that all traffic is anonymous.

3. Legal grounds and disclosure

Account, reading, paid-service and support processing fulfils the user contract; legally required transaction records are retained to meet legal duties. Optional notifications use the relevant permission/consent. We do not disclose data for independent third-party purposes without consent or another legal basis. Apple sign-in, payments and push also follow Apple’s policies. Existing purchases on other platforms also follow that platform’s retention policy and its store’s verification/refund procedures.

4. Retention and deletion

  • On deletion, account/reading/feedback data and Apple revocation tokens designated for deletion are removed promptly. Required transaction records are separated from account information; transaction identifiers may remain and are not represented as fully anonymous.
  • Contract/withdrawal and payment/supply records: 5 years; consumer complaint/dispute records: 3 years; advertising records: 6 months, as applicable under Korean law.
  • Ordinary email inquiries: deleted within 1 year after resolution. Individual Sentry error/crash events: 30 days after receipt.
  • Encrypted database backups are deleted within 7 days of creation and may temporarily retain pre-deletion data.
  • Use Delete account at the bottom of Settings. If Apple disconnection fails, the app may guide you to disconnect it directly with Apple. We cannot immediately delete independent records that Apple or another provider must retain.

5. Providers and international transfers

When the relevant function is used, data is transmitted over encrypted network connections to these providers. Authentication, database and backend storage use Seoul; overseas support access may occur. International outsourced processing/storage necessary to fulfil the contract is disclosed under Article 28-8(1)(3)(a) of Korea’s Personal Information Protection Act. Independent provider legal obligations and retention rules also apply.

6. Your rights and protection

You or an authorized representative may request access, correction, deletion or restriction through support. We request only necessary identity/authority information and explain legal retention restrictions. Access controls, encrypted transport, operating-system secure storage and encrypted backups protect data. Electronic data due for destruction is deleted using methods that prevent recovery.

7. Privacy contact and remedies

Privacy officer: Youngwan Kim, representative · studio@ddukddak.net. Korean remedies include the Privacy Infringement Report Center (privacy.kisa.or.kr, 118) and Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (kopico.go.kr, 1833-6972). Do not send passwords, verification codes or card numbers.

8. Changes

Material changes are announced through app/web notices, with separate consent where required by law. This first-release iOS policy does not change existing Android/web notices or effective dates.
