Revision notice (November 19, 2026)

Terms: The iOS app will offer a monthly subscription through App Store In-App Purchase. New section 6-2 covers payment and auto-renewal, scope, cancellation, failed payments, price changes and service termination, new section 6-3 covers cancellation and refunds, and sections 2 and 8 (account deletion) are updated. Stories saved on the device (section 6-1) do not change; stories opened only through the subscription cannot be saved. The changes take effect on November 26, 2026 and apply from the day the iOS app starts offering the subscription on or after that date. Depending on App Store review and release timing, the subscription may start later than the effective date.

The subscription renews automatically and is charged to your Apple account. Cancel in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions on your device or with Manage subscription in the app; you keep access until the end of the paid period. Apple receives and decides App Store cancellations and refunds. From the day the subscription is offered, a subscription bought through Google Play or the App Store can be used on Android, iOS and the web with the same parent account. If you subscribe through the App Store, cancel before deleting your account.

Privacy policy: Adds the data processed for the Apple monthly subscription (app-account token, subscription product/transaction IDs, price/currency, subscription status and periods, auto-renewal status, and Apple’s signed verification data and server notifications), its purposes and how payment records are kept after deletion, effective November 26, 2026.

If you do not agree to the revised terms, you can stop using the service by deleting your account in Settings before the effective date. If you do not object by then, you are treated as agreeing to the revised terms.

Revision notice (November 17, 2026)

Terms: From the next iOS app update you can save stories to the device and read them offline. New section 6-1 sets out which stories can be saved, private in-app viewing only, when saved copies open, the 30-day check and when copies are deleted. It applies from the day the iOS app starts offering device saving (corrected November 17, 2026: the first notice said November 24, 2026; because this is a newly added feature it now applies from the day the feature is offered). Copies stay in the cache area, are not included in iCloud or device backups, and may be deleted by iOS when storage runs low (you can download them again).

Privacy policy: Adds likes (stories you liked and when), with purposes, retention and how to remove them, effective November 24, 2026, and explains that saved stories stay on the device.

If you do not agree to the revised terms, you can stop using the service by deleting your account in Settings before the effective date. If you do not object by then, you are treated as agreeing to the revised terms.
