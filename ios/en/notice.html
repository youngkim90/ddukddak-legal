Revision notice (November 19, 2026)

Terms: The iOS app will offer a monthly subscription through App Store In-App Purchase. New section 5-1 covers payment and auto-renewal, scope, cancellation, failed payments and price changes, new section 5-2 covers cancellation and refunds, and sections 2 and 8 (account deletion) are updated. The changes take effect on November 26, 2026 and apply from when the iOS app starts offering the subscription on or after that date. Depending on App Store review and release timing, the subscription may start later than the effective date.

The subscription renews automatically and is charged to your Apple account. Cancel in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions on your device or with Manage subscription in the app; you keep access until the end of the paid period. Apple receives and decides App Store cancellations and refunds. From when the subscription is offered, a subscription bought through Google Play or the App Store can be used on Android, iOS and the web with the same parent account. If you subscribe through the App Store, cancel before deleting your account.

Privacy policy: Adds the data processed for the Apple monthly subscription (app-account token, subscription product/transaction IDs, price/currency, subscription status and periods, auto-renewal status, and Apple’s signed verification data and server notifications), its purposes and how payment records are kept after deletion, effective November 26, 2026.

If you do not agree to the revised terms, you can stop using the service by deleting your account in Settings before the effective date. If you do not object by then, you are treated as agreeing to the revised terms.
