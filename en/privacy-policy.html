2. Purposes

  • Account identification, authentication, signup and deletion.
  • Story delivery and saving/restoring reading progress. Encrypted Apple tokens support account linkage and authorization revocation during deletion.
  • De-identified service statistics, diagnosing errors/crashes and improving reliability. This does not mean every diagnostic event is anonymous.
  • Responding to support requests and resolving reported problems.
  • Service notifications such as newly added stories, rather than advertising or marketing notifications. Notifications can be disabled in app settings.

3. Retention and deletion

When account deletion completes, account information is destroyed without delay, subject to applicable retention exceptions and the backup rule below. Diagnostic and support records handled separately from the account are subject to the pre-publication confirmation below. In-app deletion may require retrying Apple processing; email requests involve separate identity checks and processing time. Encrypted backups are automatically deleted within seven days of creation (effective October 26, 2026).

6. Your choices and requests

You may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal information through available account settings or studio@ddukddak.net. In supported app versions, account deletion and profile editing are under Settings → Profile. See account deletion help.

7. Children and account-free child mode

The Service does not directly collect account information from children under 14 through signup. A child's guardian account is managed by a parent or legal guardian. Versions with child mode allow designated stories without an account and require the guardian check before entering account features. The same controls are not guaranteed in older versions that lack child mode.

In versions with this age flow, the entered birth date is used on the device to classify the age band; the full date is not stored or sent to the server. Only the band and mode-transition state are stored locally. This is not government-ID verification or verification of legal-guardian identity. Requests in child mode can still involve connection-information processing needed for content delivery.

If personal information from a child under 14 is found to have been collected, it is deleted immediately and the legal guardian is notified.

8. Security

  • Passwords are stored as one-way hashes.
  • HTTPS/TLS protects transmission.
  • Access to personal-information systems is restricted.
  • Token-based authentication protects sessions; Apple revocation tokens are encrypted with server access restrictions.
