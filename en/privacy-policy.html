2. Purposes

  • Account identification, authentication, signup and deletion.
  • Story delivery and saving/restoring reading progress. Encrypted Apple tokens support account linkage and authorization revocation during deletion.
  • De-identified service statistics, diagnosing errors/crashes and improving reliability. This does not mean every diagnostic event is anonymous.
  • Responding to support requests and resolving reported problems.
  • Checking for and delivering compatible app updates.
  • Service notifications such as newly added stories, rather than advertising or marketing notifications. Notifications can be disabled in app settings.

3. Retention and deletion

When account deletion completes, account information is destroyed without delay, subject to applicable retention exceptions and the backup rule below. Diagnostic and support records handled separately from the account are subject to the pre-publication confirmation below. You can check completion in the app. In-app deletion may require retrying Apple processing; email requests involve separate identity checks and processing time. Encrypted backups are automatically deleted within seven days of creation (effective October 26, 2026).

The encrypted Apple revocation token is associated with the account. During account deletion, the Service attempts authorization revocation and then deletes it with the account. The app may direct the user to revoke authorization manually when needed; a temporary Apple error may require retrying before deletion completes. Previously announced backup retention still applies.

Individual error events on the current Sentry Developer plan are retained for 30 days under the provider's policy. Account deletion does not automatically erase Sentry events; issue summaries, administrative audit records, backups and other data types do not necessarily share that period. Contact the privacy address about relevant records in a deletion request. Before a plan or retention setting changes, review the processing period and any policy amendment and notice required.

Contact details and content of general support requests and feedback are retained for up to one year after resolution to handle the request, identify follow-up inquiries about the same issue and complete follow-up actions, then destroyed. Information no longer needed for those purposes is destroyed earlier. Consumer complaint or dispute-handling records subject to the Korean Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce are retained for three years; statutory records are kept separately from general inquiries and used only for that purpose.

To confirm before publication: the one-year maximum is the operator's chosen standard. Collection notices and processing grounds, resolution dates, treatment of existing records, erasure procedures and the effective date must be checked against actual operations. Automatic deletion is not claimed to be implemented. Other Sentry data and backups, manual erasure and authentication-email retention/deletion must be established separately. Resolution of a request or deletion of an account does not promise automatic, immediate erasure of every email or log. The legal applicability of the existing retention table also remains for review with the terms work.

6. Your choices and requests

You may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal information by contacting studio@ddukddak.net. In supported app versions, account deletion and profile editing are available under Settings → Profile. See account deletion help.

6a. Deletion process — draft revision

When account deletion completes, operational account data and linked revocation tokens are deleted. Server push registrations linked to the account are also within the deletion scope. Records subject to applicable retention duties and backups are handled under their separate rules. External diagnostics and mailboxes require separate erasure handling; account-database deletion does not establish their deletion.

Before publication, establish identification of relevant records, request handling, deletion authority/confirmation and backup expiry. Provider logical deletion must not be described as immediate physical deletion of every copy.

6b. Device/browser storage and choices

Supported versions may store login sessions, account-display caches, language, age band/mode transitions, reading/audio settings and notification choices on the device. Native authentication sessions use SecureStore; web authentication uses localStorage when available. Restricting or clearing storage may limit or reset login persistence and settings. Clearing device storage does not delete the server account; use the deletion guidance in section 6.

6c. Privacy complaints and dispute resolution

In Korea, privacy complaints can be directed to the KISA Privacy Infringement Report Center (118 within Korea). The Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee can be reached at 1833-6972 within Korea.

7. Children and account-free child mode

The Service does not directly collect account information from children under 14 through signup. A child's guardian account is managed by a parent or legal guardian. Versions with child mode allow designated stories without an account and require the guardian check before entering account features. The same controls are not guaranteed in older versions that lack child mode.

In versions with this age flow, the entered birth date is used on the device to classify the age band; the full date is not stored or sent to the server. Only the band and mode-transition state are stored locally. This is not government-ID verification or verification of legal-guardian identity. In supported versions, child mode limits account diagnostics, push and app-update checks. Connection-information processing needed for content delivery can still occur. The actual network behavior of older versions, native startup and mode transitions must be verified before publication.

If personal information from a child under 14 is found to have been collected, it is deleted without undue delay and the legal guardian is notified where contact is possible. Backups and separate external records follow the retention and erasure rules in sections 3 and 6a.

8. Security

  • Passwords are stored as one-way hashes.
  • HTTPS/TLS protects transmission.
  • Access to personal-information systems is restricted.
  • Token-based authentication protects sessions; Apple revocation tokens are encrypted with server access restrictions.
