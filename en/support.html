Ddukddak Tales Support

Prepared October 25, 2026 · Pre-publication review copy

Send questions in Korean or English to studio@ddukddak.net. You do not need to sign in to email support.

What to include

  • App version, Android/iOS version and device model.
  • The screen, story title/page and selected language.
  • Steps that reproduce the problem and the error message. Hide personal details such as email addresses in screenshots.

Do not send passwords, verification codes, identity numbers or payment-card details. This page has no submission form or analytics script. Sending email involves processing by your email provider and the support process.

Login and language

In versions with language settings, you can choose Korean or English in app settings. Login methods depend on platform and app version. If you used Sign in with Apple, use the same Apple account in a supported iOS version. With Hide My Email, your account may show an Apple private relay address.

Do not enter your Apple password in the Service's email-password form. For an email account, use the password-reset option on the login screen.

Reading, audio and animation

Check Read aloud, Music and Audio settings in the reader. Audio and video may require a network connection; offline access to every story is not guaranteed. If the problem continues, send the story/page and app version. Do not erase app data or delete your account without guidance.

Delete an account

  1. Sign in to the guardian account, then open Settings → Profile → Delete account.
  2. Read the warning and confirm deletion. Reading history is not restored if you sign up again.
  3. If you cannot use the app, email a deletion request with your signup email and login method (email, Google, Kakao or Apple if applicable). Do not send passwords or verification codes.

The existing Korean deletion policy applies, including identity checks, email handling within seven business days and retention exceptions. In the app, check whether deletion has completed; Apple processing may require a retry. Email requests involve separate checks and processing time. If asked to revoke Apple authorization manually, follow Apple's instructions. You may sign up again with the same email, but deleted records will not return.

Child mode and guardian features

Versions with child mode offer designated stories without an account. Follow the app's guardian check before account, notification or support features. This check is not government-ID verification or verification of legal-guardian identity.
